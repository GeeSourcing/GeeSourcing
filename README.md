# GeeSourcing

**China sourcing, quality control, and fulfillment support for global eCommerce brands and importers.**

GeeSourcing is a sourcing and supply chain service provider based in Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. We help international buyers source products, coordinate with suppliers, manage quality inspections, and fulfill orders from China.

## What We Do

- **China Sourcing** — Supplier sourcing and product procurement based on buyer requirements.
- **Supplier Verification** — Supplier screening and verification before orders are placed.
- **Quality Control** — Product inspections and quality checks before shipment.
- **Private Label & OEM/ODM** — Support for customization, packaging, and product development.
- **Dropshipping** — China-based sourcing and order processing for eCommerce businesses.
- **Fulfillment** — Order preparation and fulfillment support from China.

## Sourcing Resources

We publish practical resources for international buyers working with Chinese suppliers, covering topics such as:
